Condition: Hypoxia
Trigger: SpO2 < 94%
Meaning: Low oxygen saturation indicating possible hypoxia
Action: Requires clinical evaluation

Condition: Severe Hypoxia
Trigger: SpO2 < 90%
Meaning: Critical oxygen deficiency
Action: Emergency intervention required

Condition: Tachycardia
Trigger: HR > 100 bpm
Meaning: Elevated heart rate indicating physiological stress
Action: Monitor for complications

Condition: Severe Tachycardia
Trigger: HR > 120 bpm
Meaning: High-risk cardiac stress condition
Action: Urgent medical evaluation required

Condition: Fetal Distress
Trigger: Reduced fetal movement
Meaning: Possible fetal compromise
Action: Immediate clinical assessment required

Condition: Critical Fetal Distress
Trigger: No fetal movement
Meaning: Severe fetal compromise
Action: Emergency intervention required

Condition: High Risk Combination
Trigger: SpO2 < 94% AND HR > 100 bpm
Meaning: Combined physiological stress condition
Action: Requires urgent attention

Condition: Critical Condition
Trigger: SpO2 < 90% AND abnormal fetal movement
Meaning: Life-threatening maternal-fetal condition
Action: Immediate emergency care required

Condition: Multi-Parameter Risk
Trigger: Multiple abnormal vital signs
Meaning: High-risk pregnancy condition
Action: Requires hospitalization

Condition: Deteriorating State
Trigger: Rapid change in vital signs
Meaning: Unstable physiological condition
Action: Emergency monitoring required

Condition: Normal State
Trigger: All vital signs within normal range
Meaning: Stable maternal condition
Action: Continue routine monitoring
