Condition: Maternal Complication Risk
Trigger: Abnormal maternal vital signs
Meaning: Indicates possible maternal health complications
Action: Requires monitoring and evaluation

Condition: Gestational Diabetes Risk
Trigger: Elevated blood glucose levels
Meaning: Hyperglycaemia during pregnancy
Action: Requires early diagnosis and management

Condition: Infection Risk
Trigger: Presence of infections (HIV, TB, urinary infection)
Meaning: Increased maternal and fetal risk
Action: Requires medical treatment

Condition: Maternal Anemia Risk
Trigger: Low hemoglobin levels
Meaning: Reduced oxygen carrying capacity
Action: Requires supplementation and treatment

Condition: Fetal Compromise
Trigger: Reduced fetal movement
Meaning: Possible fetal distress
Action: Immediate evaluation required

Condition: High Risk Pregnancy
Trigger: Multiple maternal abnormalities
Meaning: Increased risk of complications
Action: Requires higher level care

Condition: Clinical Abnormality
Trigger: Any abnormal clinical finding
Meaning: Potential health complication
Action: Prompt medical evaluation required

Condition: Combined Risk Condition
Trigger: Maternal abnormalities AND fetal distress
Meaning: High-risk maternal-fetal state
Action: Immediate intervention required

Condition: Early Detection Benefit
Trigger: Regular monitoring
Meaning: Helps detect complications early
Action: Continue routine antenatal care

Condition: Severe Outcome Risk
Trigger: Unmanaged maternal complications
Meaning: Can lead to serious maternal and fetal outcomes
Action: Immediate treatment required
